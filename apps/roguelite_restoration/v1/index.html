Trail guide & systems

Move: swipe the habitat, use the arrow pad, or use WASD / arrow keys. Walk onto litter, soil, sprouts or springs to interact.

Act: select a tool and tap RESTORE or press Space/E. Cleaning produces supplies; planting consumes them; watering uses finite canteen water. Blue springs have only two safe draws each.

Quests: complete prerequisites in order, then step onto the gold passage in the bottom-right corner. After sector one, select a headwaters or canopy route. Three sectors make a complete trail.

Model: soil, water and biodiversity are bounded, simplified game indicators—not ecological measurements or field advice. Every three successful actions causes a small drought penalty.

Persistence: IndexedDB is preferred, localStorage is a limited fallback, memory-only mode does not survive reloads. A downloaded backup is portable. A service worker enables hosted offline reloads only after successful online installation.

Keys: 1–4 choose tools. Enter on the golden passage can also advance. Esc dismisses the route dialog so you can choose later.
